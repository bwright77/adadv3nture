# WLW Fueling & Long-Run Protocol

> The standing fueling reference for the West Line Winder 30K block (Sep 26, 2026).
> Source: Jun 9 2026 coaching handoff. Fuel rate (g carbs/hr) is a **primary
> objective** of every long run — tracked like pace. The app surfaces the per-week
> target + standing formula in the briefing and export; this doc is the full protocol.

---

## The core principle

GLP-1 (since Nov 2024) suppresses appetite. The failures so far were **starting
depleted**, not in-run absorption. So: **eat on the clock, not on hunger.** Start
full. The drink mix carries the bulk of the hourly target (cheapest per gram); gels
supplement; fruit/real food is early-and-low-intensity only (fructose-heavy, caps
the rate).

## Pre-run / daily fueling

GLP-1 suppresses appetite, so the protocol is calendar-driven, not hunger-driven:

- **Race week / day-before / race morning:** ~**100–150g carbs across the 3 hours
  pre-start**, finished **60–90 min out**.
- Eat on the clock the day before and race morning so you **start full**.
- The in-run target then maintains, rather than digs out of, a deficit.

  matters in a race.

## Form & back notes

- **Low-mid back tightness** (thoracolumbar, vest zone) on long runs. Mitigations:
  vest high/tight + front-load flasks, don't over-carry water, build vest tolerance,
  Row Bootcamp (rowed clean) + anti-extension isometrics (planks, bird dogs, Pallof,
  carries).
- **Cue stack** ("reset," head-to-toe, preceded by a shake-out): relax neck → chin
  level, ears over shoulders → shoulders back **and down** → engage core, ribs down →
  gentle pelvic tuck → stack it up → spread toes. Full stack at easy moments; single
  trigger ("stack it up" / "ribs down") on technical ground. **Tie resets to fueling
  sips — one ritual, two jobs.**
- **Knee drive / heel kick:** leave natural at trail pace; "pick the feet up" when
  fatigued on technical ground; snappy mechanics live in strides only.
